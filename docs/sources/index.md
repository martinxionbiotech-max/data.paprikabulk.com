# Sources

Every technical statement on this site should trace to one of the sources below. Where a standard exists in more than one edition, the edition matters — the current edition is named, and superseded editions are marked as such.

## Standards — colour, moisture and physical methods

| Identifier | Title | Issuer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO 7541:2020** | Spices and condiments — Spectrophotometric determination of the extractable colour in paprika | [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/70872.html) | **Current edition** of the paprika colour standard |
| **ISO 7541:1989** | Ground (powdered) paprika — Determination of total natural colouring matter content | [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/14324.html) | **Withdrawn** — superseded by ISO 7541:2020. Cited here only where historical equivalence with older COAs matters |
| **ASTA Method 20.1** | Determination of Extractable Color in Capsicums and Their Oleoresins | [ASTA](https://astaspice.org/resources/asta-method-20-1-determination-of-extractable-color-in-capsicums-and-their-oleoresins/) | The industry method most COAs quote as "ASTA colour value" |
| **ISO 939** | Spices and condiments — Determination of moisture content | ISO | Entrainment (Dean–Stark type) method |
| **ISO 972:1997** | Chillies and capsicums, whole or ground (powdered) — Specification | [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/5408.html) | Product specification for chillies/capsicums |
| **ASTM E11** | Standard Specification for Woven Wire Test Sieve Cloth and Test Sieves | [ASTM](https://www.astm.org/e0011-22.html) | Mesh designation basis for particle-size work |
| **ISO 3310-1** | Test sieves — Technical requirements and testing — Part 1 | ISO | Sieve verification |
| **ISO 2591-1** | Test sieving — Methods using test sieves of woven wire cloth and perforated metal plate | ISO | Test-sieving procedure |
| **ASTM D7481** | Standard Test Methods for Determining Loose and Tapped Bulk Densities of Powders | [ASTM](https://www.astm.org/d7481-18.html) | Bulk density of powders |
| **ISO 760 / ASTM E1064** | Water content — Karl Fischer method | ISO / ASTM | Reference method for moisture disputes |

**ASTA and ASTM are different organisations with different scopes.** ASTM E-series and D-series methods cover test methodology (sieves, bulk density, dust-explosion parameters, packaging seals); ASTA methods cover the analytical methods of the spice trade (colour, sensory heat). A colour value is **ASTA 20.1**, never "ASTM 20.1".

## How to use this register

1. Match the identifier quoted on a certificate or datasheet to the table above.
2. Check the **edition** — a COA quoting "ISO 7541" without a year is ambiguous after the 2020 revision.
3. When a lab reports a method you cannot match, ask for the full method reference before accepting the number.
